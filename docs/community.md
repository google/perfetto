# Community and getting help

## Ask a question

The best place to ask is the **#help** channel on our
[Discord server](https://discord.gg/35ShE3A). The Perfetto team and community
answer there, usually within a few hours. Include your Perfetto version, the
platform you are tracing and, if you can, the trace itself.

If you prefer email, you can also ask on our
[public mailing list](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/perfetto-dev).

**Googlers**: use Discord too, unless your question involves internal-only
details. For those, use [YAQS](https://go/perfetto-yaqs) or our
[internal mailing list](http://go/perfetto-dev). If your enquiry is truly
urgent see [this page](http://go/perfetto-project).

## Report a bug

For bugs affecting any part of Perfetto **except** Chrome tracing, use
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/google/perfetto/issues).

For bugs affecting Chrome tracing, use http://crbug.com
`Component:Speed>Tracing label:Perfetto`.

**Googlers**: use the internal bug tracker
[go/perfetto-bugs](http://goto.google.com/perfetto-bugs).

## Follow development

- **Releases** are announced in the #announcements channel on
  [Discord](https://discord.gg/35ShE3A), on
  [GitHub releases](https://github.com/google/perfetto/releases) and on the
  [mailing list](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/perfetto-dev).
- **Design proposals (RFCs)** are discussed in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/google/perfetto/discussions/categories/team-design-discussions).
  New RFCs, issues and discussions are also posted to the #github channel on
  Discord.
- To contribute code, see [Contributing](/docs/contributing/getting-started.md).
